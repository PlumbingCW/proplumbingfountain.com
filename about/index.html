How we work

What you can expect

  • A clear price before work starts.
  • Straight answers about timing when you call.
  • A plumber you can check through the state license lookup.

We tell you when a job is outside our scope. For example, exterior foundation waterproofing is foundation work, and we say so on our basement water control page.

What happens when you call

From ring to repair

  1. A dispatcher answers and asks where you are, what is happening and whether the water is stopped.
  2. We tell you which valve to close and what to move out of the way.
  3. A plumber comes to the address, finds the cause and states the price before work starts.
  4. We do the work, test it and explain what failed.

We would rather spend a few minutes on the phone getting the details right than send someone out blind. A good description of the symptom often decides which parts go on the truck.

Why Fountain water shapes our work

Fountain Utilities publishes its water hardness. It runs about 165 ppm from November through April and about 350 ppm from May through October. That is hard to very hard water, and it leaves scale in heaters, on fixtures and inside pipes.

We see the results in our calls: heaters that run out of hot water early, aerators that clog and softener questions. Our water softener and filtration page explains what treatment does and does not do.

Licensing in Colorado

According to the Colorado Department of Regulatory Agencies, plumbing in Colorado is regulated by the State Plumbing Board. You can verify any plumber's license through DORA's online lookup, and you should for any contractor you hire. Permit rules are set by the local authority. In Fountain that is the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department, reachable at 719-327-2880.

How we explain a repair

A repair that nobody explains is hard to trust. After the work, we tell you what failed and why, what we replaced and what to watch for. If a related problem is likely to follow, we say so plainly and leave the decision to you.

That includes saying when a cheaper option makes sense. A tank that still has life can sometimes be repaired, and a pipe with one leak does not always need a full repipe.

Pricing without surprises

What goes into a price

The problem itself
A clogged drain, a failed heater and a burst pipe each need different work.
Access
A fixture in the open costs less to reach than a pipe behind a finished wall.
Parts
We tell you what parts the job needs and why.
Hour of the call
Nights, weekends and holidays are priced for the hour, and we state the figure first.
Permits
Where a permit is required, such as for water heater work, it is part of the plan.

You hear the number before we start. If we find something new once the work is open, we stop, explain and ask before changing it.
