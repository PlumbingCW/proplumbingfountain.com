What to do while you wait

In the first few minutes

  • Close the shutoff at the fixture, or the main valve if the source is unclear.
  • Keep clear of water near outlets, panels and plugged-in appliances.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.
  • Move belongings off wet floors and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Then call. The dispatcher will ask a few questions so the plumber arrives with the right parts.

Fountain water and your plumbing

Fountain Utilities publishes its water hardness. It runs about 165 ppm, or 10 grains per gallon, from November through April and about 350 ppm, or 20 grains per gallon, from May through October. Water at that level leaves scale in water heaters, on fixtures and inside pipes.

That is a big part of why water heater calls are so common here. Read water heater repair to see what scale does to a tank, and water softener and filtration if you want to treat the water itself.
