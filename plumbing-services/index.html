Emergency work and planned work

Some jobs cannot wait. A burst pipe, a sewer backup, a failed water heater or a pump that stopped during a storm are emergency calls, and our line is open around the clock. Pages for those jobs carry an emergency label in the title.

Other jobs are planned. Water heater installation, repiping, a new bathroom, a softener or basement water control are scheduled around your calendar. You get a price before work starts either way.

Where to start

Matching the symptom to a page

  • Slow or backed-up drains: drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning.
  • Cold showers or a leaking tank: water heater repair and replacement.
  • Unexplained wet spots or a high bill: leak detection.
  • Water spraying from a pipe: burst pipe repair.
  • Water in the basement: sump pump repair and basement water control.

How pricing works

We state the price before work starts. The plumber looks at the problem, explains what is wrong and what the repair involves, and you decide. Each service page lists the factors that move the number, such as access, parts, the hour of the call and permits.

We do not publish prices online, because a number that ignores your house is a guess. According to a 2025 Colorado State Plumbing Board bulletin, water heater work also needs a permit and an inspection, and that belongs in any honest price. Call (719) 530-4349 and describe the problem, and we will tell you how we price it.
