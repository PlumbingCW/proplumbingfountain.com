Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect when you call us or visit this website, and how we use it. Last updated November 14, 2026.

Information we collect

When you call us, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us, such as your name, address and the plumbing problem. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This website does not have forms or accounts. Our hosting provider may log technical details such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed.

How we use it

We use call information to schedule and perform work, give you a price, follow up on service and improve how we answer calls. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with the people who need it to do the job, such as our dispatchers and technicians, and where the law requires it.

Your choices

Tell us at the start of a call if you do not want it recorded. To ask about information we hold, email contact@proplumbingfountain.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
