What to do first when plumbing fails here

Before we arrive

  • Shut off the water at the fixture valve or at the main, whichever is closer and easier to turn.
  • If water is near outlets, a panel or a plugged-in appliance, stay out of it and switch off the breaker only if you can reach it dry.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics off wet floors, and take photos for your insurer.

Tell the dispatcher your cross streets, what is leaking and whether the water is stopped. That lets us bring the right parts the first time.

A community split by Fontaine Boulevard

Security and Widefield grew as separate neighborhoods, and the two names are still used on maps, school boundaries and mailing addresses. Fontaine Boulevard marks the line between them. Widefield borders Fountain, so many callers on that side are only a short hop from our own street.

Who supplies your water

Water service in the community is split among local districts, and that matters when a pipe leaks before your meter. Security Water District, reachable at 719-392-3475, serves Security. Widefield Water and Sanitation District, at 719-390-7111, serves Widefield. If a leak is on the utility side of the meter, they are the right call. Everything after the meter is yours, and that is where we work.

The Security district reports drawing about 66 percent of its supply from Widefield aquifer wells, about 24 percent from the Fountain Valley Authority and about 5 percent from the Southern Delivery System. The mix can change, so ask your district for current figures before you buy treatment equipment.

PFAS, treatment plants and your plumbing

ATSDR attributes PFAS in the area's groundwater to firefighting foam used at Peterson Air Force Base. The compounds were detected in Widefield in 2013 and 2014, in Security in 2014 and in the Security mobile home park in 2016. The Security district's ion-exchange treatment plant went online in April 2021, and ATSDR says the water now meets federal guidelines.

That is a utility matter and we do not test water. What we can do is explain filter options, install a system you have chosen and keep your fixtures working. See water softener and filtration for how treatment fits into a home.
