Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Fountain, CO

When the main sewer line stops, the lowest drain in the house usually shows it first, often a basement floor drain or a tub, and our crews are available 24/7 to clear it. Stop using water now and keep the cleanout cap closed until the plumber tells you otherwise.

Call (719) 530-4349

Sewer line cleaning is different from a kitchen snake job. The main line carries every fixture's waste to the city connection, so a stoppage can sit anywhere from the house to the property line. The work starts at the cleanout and uses a heavier machine than a sink needs.

This page explains how the main line is cleared, how we tell a recurring cause from a one-off, and when cleaning alone is not enough. For single-fixture problems use drain cleaning, and for repeat roots or grease see hydro jetting.

Sewer backup: the first hour

A main line backup has a signature. Several fixtures misbehave together, the toilet gurgles when the washer drains, and the lowest drain in the house is the first to overflow. If that is what you see, assume the main line.

Do this before we arrive

  • Stop all water use, including toilets and laundry.
  • Find the cleanout and clear a path to it, but leave the cap on.
  • Keep everyone out of standing sewage and ventilate the area if you can do so safely.
  • Note which fixtures are affected, because that tells us where along the line the stoppage likely sits.

We run this service at night and on weekends. The dispatcher can tell you what to expect when you call.

How a main line is cleaned

From cleanout to clear flow

  1. We open the cleanout, which is often a capped pipe near the foundation, and check what drains out.
  2. A sewer machine with a cutting head goes in on a heavy cable and works toward the blockage.
  3. We pull the cable, remove debris and run the machine back and forth until flow is steady.
  4. A camera confirms what caused the stoppage, so you leave with an answer and not just a clear drain.

If no cleanout exists, we can work through a pulled toilet or a roof vent, and we recommend installing a cleanout while the line is open. It makes every future visit faster.

Sewer cleaning in Fountain homes

Fountain Utilities delivers water at about 165 ppm hardness in winter and about 350 ppm in summer, and the mineral film that hard water leaves is one reason cast iron lines can narrow over the years. A heavier scale layer gives grease and paper a place to catch, so a main line that has never been cleaned can cause a stoppage with little warning.

Main line problems show up in older neighborhoods around Downtown Fountain and in newer streets in Lorson Ranch and Mesa Ridge alike. The cause can be roots, scale, grease, a settled joint or debris, and a camera tells us which. Properties near Fountain Creek can also have wetter soil around the line.

Where cleaning ends and code work begins

Cleaning a line is maintenance. Replacing or rerouting pipe is code work. Model codes such as the IPC and UPC require cleanouts so a line can be reached, and when a camera shows a missing one, adding it is a common part of the next repair. Your building department can tell you what applies at your address.
