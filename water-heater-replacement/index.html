Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Fountain, CO

When a tank has split or flooded a utility room, you need a replacement quickly, and our line is open 24/7 for that call. Shut the cold water valve on top of the heater, switch off the gas or the breaker, and leave the standing water for us if it is near electrical outlets.

Call (719) 530-4349

A failed tank is one of the few plumbing problems where the right answer is often a new unit, not a patch. The steel shell rusts through from the inside, usually after years of heating cycles, and there is no repair that makes that safe again. We explain what is going on, give you options and explain timing on the call, since it depends on the unit and the day.

If you are still not sure the tank is the problem, start with water heater repair. If you want a larger change such as moving the heater or going tankless, see water heater installation and tankless water heaters.

A flooding tank: your first ten minutes

What to do while we are on the way

  1. Close the cold water valve on the pipe above the heater to stop new water flowing in.
  2. Turn the gas control to off, or switch the heater's breaker off at the panel.
  3. If water is spreading toward the rest of the house, use a mop or towels to protect flooring and move stored items.
  4. Open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure if the dispatcher suggests it, and photograph the tank and the water for your records.

We handle tank failures at night and on weekends. The dispatcher confirms the size and fuel type with you so the right unit is on the truck.

Permit, inspection and licensed work

The Colorado State Plumbing Board said in a 2025 bulletin that water heater installation or replacement requires a permit and inspection by the authority having jurisdiction, performed by a licensed plumber working for a registered plumbing contractor. In Fountain the authority is the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department, reachable at 719-327-2880.

That rule protects you. An unpermitted swap can hold up a home sale and can create a gas or venting hazard nobody has inspected. Ask your plumber who pulls the permit and when the inspection happens.

What shortens the life of a tank here

Fountain Utilities reports hardness of about 165 ppm in winter and about 350 ppm in summer. Scale builds on the bottom of a tank, and the resulting heat trapped under sediment accelerates rust. A heater left unflushed for years in hard water can fail earlier than one in a soft water area.

When we replace your heater, we explain flushing and anode rod checks. A softener is a bigger decision, covered on our water softener and filtration page. If your home is in a newer neighborhood such as Lorson Ranch or Mesa Ridge, ask whether a builder or manufacturer warranty still applies before you buy.

Codes and listings for a new tank

A replacement has to follow the manufacturer's instructions and the plumbing and fuel gas codes the local authority enforces. Gas piping and venting are addressed by a widely used standard, the National Fuel Gas Code, NFPA 54, which an inspector may reference. The 2025 state bulletin makes the permit and inspection mandatory.
